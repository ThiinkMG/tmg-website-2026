The Practical Questions

What clients ask first.

How do we begin?

Reach out by email or phone. We'll respond within one business day with a short brief and a calendar link. After a 60-minute discovery, you receive a written proposal within seven days.

Payment terms?

50% on signature, 50% on delivery. We accept credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Afterpay. Long-form engagements can be split into milestones.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — Print Craft Creations ships worldwide. Cards, books, ebooks, branded merchandise. Rates calculated at checkout or on quote approval.

How many rounds of revision?

Two for Foundation, three for Architecture, unlimited within scope for Legacy. Rounds are substantive — we don't count one-pixel adjustments as a round.

Do you take rush work?

Occasionally, with a 35% surcharge. We'd rather decline a fast job than ship one we're not proud of in a decade.

Who owns the work?

You do. On final payment, all final files, source artwork, and brand documentation transfer to you in full — no licensing strings, no asset hostages.
